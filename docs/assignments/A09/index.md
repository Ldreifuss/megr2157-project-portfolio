# A9 – Pulley System Design

## First Steps
I started by writing down the parameters, knowns, and decisions to make for the given problem. I decided to hold off on any decisions before I needed to (which I assumed would be in the process of selecting a belt). 

In addition, (although not required for the assignment) I created a solidworks model of the pulley in order to better visualize the problem. I decided that the material for the pulley would be ABS, because that was the material that I could find in Solidworks without different types.
